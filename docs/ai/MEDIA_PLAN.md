### Curation rules (phase 01b)
- **Per project:** 1 opening (landscape 3:2, exterior, the strongest), 6–12 gallery images (mixing exterior, interior and detail, ≥ 1 portrait 4:5), and 1 "card" image for lists and the Accordion.
- **Exclude:** drawings/plans from the gallery (they go to the optional Drawings section only if clean), near-duplicates, and images < 1400 px on the long side for openings.
- **Prefer:** frames that show the language (archetypal volume + landscape + diffuse light) over generic interiors.
- **Tooling:** generate a contact sheet per folder (`scripts/contact-sheet.py`) and pick by eye. Record picks in `images.config.json`.

## MED-H1 / MED-H2 — Hero photo + foreground cutout

- **Onde:** `/` · hero
- **Função:** show the language in < 10 s and carry the "N O R D" letters behind the house.
- **Tipo:** photo plus an alpha cutout of the foreground (house).
- **Resolution:** 2400×2300 after sky extension (source 3000×2000). Encode at 1280 / 1920 / 2400 (native width; never upscale).
- **Safe area:**
  - The top ~30% must be sky, mid-to-dark in value (dusk), for the white letters.
  - The roof line sits at `roofLine = 908/2300 ≈ 0.395` of the image height. This feeds `object-position` so the roof lands at ~23.5% of the viewport.
- **Luminance check:** mean relative luminance of the letter zone ≤ 0.30, and of the label zone ≤ 0.18. Measured by `images.mjs` into the manifest.
- **Sky extension:** the prototype stretched the top 200 px band to +700 px with a feathered seam (`_explore/motion/img/hero-x.jpg`). Re-do it at full resolution from the original. Accept it if there is no visible seam; otherwise choose a photo with natural sky headroom.
- **Cutout:** `rembg` (`isnet-general-use`), then check the edges at the roof, the eaves and the semi-transparent glass pavilion. Manual touch-up if a halo is visible. Same canvas as the photo (pixel-aligned).
- **Origem:** cliente-tem (study content), credited.

### Fallback
If the cutout edge is not clean, the top row sits fully in front of the photo (no depth), and the hero keeps everything else.

## Consistência entre assets

Every photo used shares, by curation:

- **Light:** diffuse (overcast, haze, low sun, dusk). Avoid hard midday light.
- **Material:** the two poles of dark timber or metal and pale render, concrete or raw pine.
- **Composition:** the house sits in its landscape; frontal or slightly oblique; low horizon; generous sky.
- **Dominant palette (from photos):** `#F2F0EB`, `#D6B98E`, `#BDB8AF`, `#8A847A`, `#2A2826`, `#7C8A4E`, `#B48A52`, with sky `#DCE1E6`.
- **Never shown:** visible people (except passers-by when unavoidable), ornamental or historic architecture, urban clutter in openings, watermarks, plans inside the gallery flow.
