## The project in one sentence

Nord Arch — a **fictional** residential architecture studio based in Santa Catarina (BR) — needs a multi-page gallery site where every built work leads to a WhatsApp conversation about that exact work. Success is a visitor who arrives **citing a specific project**.

This is a **personal concept study by Coded by M**. It runs locally only, is never deployed, and uses third-party photos from Divisare as study content, with credits kept in the data.

## Who it is for

A high-income couple building their own house. They are experts: they arrive with a plot, a programme and references. They do not need educating. What they are afraid of is that the studio's **aesthetic language** does not match theirs. The proof they accept is the built work.

## What the visitor must do

- **Primary action:** open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message naming the project (`wa.me`, placeholder number).
- **Where it appears:**
  - the Tear Ticket at the close of `/` (variant `home`)
  - the close of every `/projetos/:slug` (variant `project`, the main CTA of the site)
  - `/contato` (variant `contact`)
  - a quiet "WhatsApp" item in the header nav

There is no floating WhatsApp bubble.

## Tone

- **Register:** brand
- **Voice:**
  - *Quiet*: "Casas que pertencem ao lugar antes de pertencerem a nós."
  - *Precise*: "Pleat House · Rancho Queimado · 2024 · 310 m²"
  - *Confident without selling*: "Conversar sobre a Pleat House."
- **Never sound like:**
  - a service provider ("orçamento grátis!", "fale com um especialista")
  - inspirational filler ("transformando sonhos em realidade")
  - AI copy (contrast reveals, negation lists, "não é só X, é Y")

## Visual direction in one sentence

*A white, silent gallery where Nord Arch's architecture is the only thing that speaks. A rigid asymmetric grid, restrained extended type, and slow, heavy motion (like light moving across a volume) used only where it reveals, connects or invites.*

## Non-negotiable constraints

- **Colour and form:**
  - Palette is `#FFFFFF` / `#0B0B0C` / `#6B6E72` / `#E4E6E8`, with **no accent colour**. Colour comes only from the photos.
  - Radius 0 and no shadows.
  - No gradient, scrim or text-shadow over photos.
- **Type:** Michroma 400 for display, nav, labels and manifesto; Aktiv Grotesk 400/500 for body. Uppercase with tracking only in the wordmark and the hero letters.
- **Logo angle:** the 33.7° roof diagonal of the logo is the only non-orthogonal form, used in reveals, page wipes and the tear.
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA, and every effect honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Performance:**
  - LCP < 2.5 s · CLS < 0.05 · INP < 200 ms
  - initial JS ≤ 250 KB gzip
  - hero photo + cutout ≤ 450 KB (AVIF)
- **Content honesty:**
  - No fabricated social proof: no testimonials, awards, client logos or numbers.
  - Every project carries `fictional: true` and a `credit` block.
- **Delivery:** site language pt-BR, local only, `noindex,nofollow`, a "estudo conceitual" notice and a `/creditos` page.
