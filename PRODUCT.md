This is a portfolio surface: the impression *is* the product. The site has to embody Nord Arch's architectural language, not describe it.

## What it is

Nord Arch is a multi-page gallery site for a fictional residential architecture studio based in Santa Catarina, Brazil. The studio is full-service: architecture, interiors, landscape and construction management, with commercial work as a minor line.

Its job is to let an expert couple recognise an aesthetic language in under 10 seconds, fall for one specific house, and open a WhatsApp conversation about that house.

It is a **personal concept study** by Coded by M. It is not published and runs locally only. The photos are third-party (Divisare), used as study content with credits.

## Audience

The audience is a **high-income couple building their own house**.

- **What they bring:** they already have a plot, a programme and references. They compare studios by language and rigour, not by price lists.
- **How they decide:** together, over weeks, sharing links with each other.
- **What they need:** no education. Their single question is: *"does this studio's language match ours?"*

## Purpose / goals

- **Primary:** modernise the studio's digital presence through an authorial portfolio. The conversion is **WhatsApp, pre-filled with the project's name and code**.
- **Secondary:** show the portfolio (12 works, NA—01…NA—12).
- **Success signal:** visitors arrive citing a specific project ("vi a Pleat House…").

## Voice

The voice is **quiet, precise, editorial, confident and unhurried**.

- Short declarative sentences.
- Concrete nouns: material, light, place.
- Project names as proper nouns, with ficha técnica precision.
- It never sells. It states what the work is and lets the photos argue.

## Anti-references

- **Explicit veto from the developer: "IA slop".** No gradient text, aurora blobs, glass cards, three-column icon grids, generic fade-up-on-everything, or "Saiba mais →" on cards. No contrast-reveal copy ("não é só X, é Y").
- **Service-provider patterns seen in Santa Catarina competitors:**
  - a floating green WhatsApp bubble
  - a Google-reviews widget
  - price FAQ and city-list SEO pages
  - a client-area mixed into the showcase
- **Template-architect sites:** a hero slider with arrows, a Wix/Squarespace grid with no year or place, and project pages with photos but no concept.
- **Fabricated proof:** testimonials, awards, client logos and "+200 obras" numbers. None exist.
- **Demo-looking motion:** React Bits components with their default dark overlay, 14–16 px radius or default speeds.
